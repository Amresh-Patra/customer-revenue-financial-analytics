# E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Financial Analytics Project

## Executive Summary

This project is a comprehensive Business Intelligence and Financial Analytics case study built using SQL and real-world business analysis frameworks.

Rather than focusing only on SQL syntax, the project simulates how a Data Analyst supports business decision-making by transforming raw transactional data into actionable financial, marketing, customer, and operational insights.

The project contains **48 business-focused SQL analyses** covering revenue growth, profitability, cost optimization, customer retention, marketing ROI, customer lifetime value, churn risk, and executive-level financial reporting.

Each analysis follows a structured analytical workflow:

**Business Problem → Business Objective → KPI Definition → SQL Solution → Business Interpretation**

The objective is not simply to answer data questions, but to demonstrate the ability to connect data with business outcomes and strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Questions Addressed

This project answers critical business questions such as:

### Revenue Intelligence

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* How is revenue changing month-over-month and year-over-year?
* What are the strongest and weakest performing sales periods?

### Profitability Intelligence

* Which categories generate the highest profit margins?
* How do discounts affect profitability?
* What are the largest sources of revenue leakage?

### Marketing Intelligence

* Which acquisition channels generate the highest ROI?
* What is the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by channel and region?
* How efficiently is marketing spend converted into revenue?

### Customer Intelligence

* What is the customer retention rate?
* Which customers generate the highest lifetime value?
* Which customers are at risk of churn?
* How concentrated is revenue across the customer base?

### Executive Financial Intelligence

* What is the true net revenue after all costs?
* Which regions generate the highest profitability?
* What are the key financial health indicators of the business?
